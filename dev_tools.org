#+TITLE: Dev Tools
#+STARTUP: fold

Simple org copy of [[file:dev_tools.rec][dev_tools.rec]]. The recfile is the source;
layout fields (Pos, Size, Kind, Fit, Color) are left out.

The list is ordered: of two tools that share a tag, the one written first
is preferred. The NEEDS property lists the prerequisites (edges into a tool):
all of the comma-separated items are needed; "a or b" (an Any group in the
rec file) needs only one of them.
