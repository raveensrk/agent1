* AXIOM lemmax names the workflow; lmx names the program
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:          01a20d23-eb54-73d6-a29f-4796fa7d0327
:CREATED:     2026-11-24
:LAST_EDITED: 2026-11-24

When the owner says lemmax, or LemmaX, it means the LemmaX project or the workflow. When the owner says lmx, it means lmx, the program that supports the workflow. Terms: [[file:../lemmax/theory.definitions.org::*LemmaX][LemmaX]] and [[file:../lemmax/theory.definitions.org::*lmx][lmx]].
